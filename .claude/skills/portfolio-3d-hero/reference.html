Patrimonio

A personal finance app that measures net worth, not just cash flow: what you own, minus what you owe.

  • Net worth registry: assets against debts, and liquid money against what is locked in.
  • Financial health: savings rate, months of emergency fund and debt-to-income, each with a plain verdict.
  • Multi-currency: every movement keeps the currency it happened in; totals convert to COP.
  • Two-step login with an authenticator app.
